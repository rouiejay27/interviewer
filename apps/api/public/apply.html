Before you start

    Check your microphone

    Click the button, allow microphone access, then say a few words. The bar should move.

    Use a quiet place. Headphones help prevent echo. Chrome or Edge on a computer, or Chrome or Safari on a phone, work best.

    Your agreement

    No video or audio recording is stored. You can end the interview at any time.

    Connecting...

    Answer in your own words. You can say "can you repeat the question" or "let me think for a moment" at any time. If you lose connection, contact the recruiter; a dropped call is not counted against you.
